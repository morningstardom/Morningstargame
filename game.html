How to play

Tap a station to walk there and do its job. Tap several in a row to line up moves, or tap an order ticket to head to its next station.

Screen print: Sales → Receiving → Screen Room → Press → Dryer → Finishing → Shipping.
Embroidery and Heat press skip screens and the dryer.

The dryer and embroidery machine run on their own. Pull loads when they flash green. Leave shirts in the dryer too long and they scorch.

On press and heat press, tap PULL in the gold zone for a perfect print and a bigger payout.

Ship fast for bigger tips. If a customer's bar runs out, they cancel. Spend your earnings on equipment and put coworkers on the schedule to cover stations.
